§02The Floor Printed · sealed · kept

The RM100 floor is not a slogan. It's a print job.

Every box is packed against live Malaysian retail prices, then sealed with a printed Value Manifest — the receipt-style card you'll find inside, declaring every item and what it's worth on the day it was packed.

If the declared total isn't at least RM100, that box never ships. If we're ever wrong, we replace it — no questions.

Worst case, you break even in goods. Best case, you pull the grail. That's the whole business, and it's why our boxes get opened on live TikTok pulls every night.

§04Questions Fair questions · straight answers

Ask the awkward ones.

Is the Maggi thing a joke?

The joke is real. 100 packets is RM130 at shop prices, which clears the RM100 floor, so it's a legitimate prize — and we will genuinely ship you a carton of Maggi. You didn't lose money. You just didn't win a phone.

So most boxes are groceries?

Yes. 9,500 of every 10,000 boxes are the dapur tier. We printed that number in §01 instead of burying it, because a guarantee you can't check isn't a guarantee.

Then why would I buy groceries for RM100?

You wouldn't. You're buying the ticket to the 1-in-10,000 — and unlike a lottery ticket, this one still pays for itself in real goods when it loses.

How do you calculate "RM100 of value"?

Current Malaysian retail price — what the same item sells for new here today, not an inflated "RRP". Every item and its price is printed on the manifest inside your box, with a verify link.

What if my box opens under RM100?

Then we broke our own rule — we replace the box free, no questions, and you keep the original. The floor check happens before sealing, so it shouldn't happen; the policy exists so you never have to take our word for it.

Shipping & returns?

Tracked shipping nationwide in 1–3 working days. Sealed boxes can't be returned once opened (that's the game) — but damaged or under-floor boxes are replaced free. Bulky dapur prizes ship as a separate carton.
